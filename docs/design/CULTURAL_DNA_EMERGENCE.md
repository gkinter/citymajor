# IRON & OAK: Cultural DNA + Geography = Emergent City Identity

## The System Where Player Decisions Create Organic City Archetypes

---

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY

The player never selects "tourist city" or "tech hub" from a menu. Instead, every city identity **emerges** from three intersecting forces:

1. **Geography** -- What the map gives you (climate, terrain, resources)
2. **Cultural DNA** -- Who your founding population is and how they evolve
3. **Player Policy** -- What laws, investments, and infrastructure you build

A tropical island with relaxed citizens, good safety laws, and beach development **becomes** a Tourist Paradise. A continental mountain city with disciplined workers, union laws, and heavy industry **becomes** an Industrial Powerhouse. The game recognizes these patterns and rewards them with bonuses, challenges, and visual transformations.

## 2. GEOGRAPHIC TRAIT SYSTEM

### 2.1 Climate Types (1 per map)

| Climate | Temperature | Seasons | Risks | Opportunities |
|---------|------------|---------|-------|--------------|
| **Tropical** | Hot year-round, humid | Wet/dry | Hurricanes, flooding, disease | Tourism, tropical agriculture, year-round construction |
| **Subtropical** | Warm, mild winters | 4 mild seasons | Hurricanes, heat waves | Tourism, agriculture, retirement destination |
| **Temperate** | Moderate, 4 distinct seasons | Full 4 seasons | Occasional storms | Balanced agriculture, diverse economy |
| **Continental** | Hot summers, harsh winters | Extreme 4 seasons | Blizzards, heat waves | Agriculture (summer), industry, resilience culture |
| **Mediterranean** | Dry hot summers, mild wet winters | 2 seasons | Drought, wildfires | Wine/olive agriculture, tourism, outdoor lifestyle |
| **Arid/Desert** | Extreme heat, cold nights | Hot/mild | Drought, sandstorms, water scarcity | Solar energy, mining, unique tourism |
| **Arctic/Subarctic** | Extreme cold, short summers | Long winter, brief summer | Permafrost, isolation, SAD | Mining, oil/gas, geothermal, midnight sun tourism |
| **Oceanic** | Mild, cloudy, rainy | Muted 4 seasons | Persistent rain, coastal storms | Fishing, port trade, green energy (wind) |

| Terrain | Buildable Land | Resources | Special Mechanics |
|---------|---------------|-----------|------------------|
| **Coastal** | Moderate | Fish, salt, sand, port access | Beach tourism, tsunami/storm surge risk, naval trade |
| **Island** | Limited | Fish, tropical fruit, coral, isolation | Space constraint, unique ecosystems, ferry/air dependent |
| **River Valley** | Good (flat) | Fertile soil, freshwater, gravel | Flooding risk, river trade, hydropower, bridges needed |
| **Mountain** | Limited (steep) | Iron, coal, copper, gold, gems | Mining, ski tourism, altitude health, engineering challenge |

| **Peninsula** | Moderate | Port access, fish, strategic | Strategic port, limited road access, naval importance |
| **Volcanic** | Moderate | Geothermal energy, fertile volcanic soil | Eruption risk (rare but catastrophic), hot springs tourism |
| **Canyon/Gorge** | Limited | Hydropower, minerals, scenic views | Bridge engineering, tourism, difficult transport routing |

### 3.1 Eight Cultural Dimensions

Each city's population has 8 cultural values on a 0-100 scale:

| **Risk Tolerance** | Conservative, risk-averse | Entrepreneurial, adventurous | Low: Traditional rural / High: Silicon Valley, Dubai |

| **Cultural Pride** | Cosmopolitan, globalized | Strong local identity, insular | Low: Singapore, NYC / High: France, Japan, India |

### 3.3 How Cultural DNA Affects Gameplay

| Dimension | Affects |
|-----------|---------|
| **Work Ethic** | Productivity per worker, overtime acceptance, demand for leisure/parks, work-life balance protests |

| **Risk Tolerance** | Startup rate, R&D investment enthusiasm, acceptance of experimental tech, venture capital attraction |

## 4. EMERGENT CITY ARCHETYPES

### 4.1 How Emergence Works

Every game month, the system evaluates conditions and calculates an **archetype score** for each of 20 possible identities. When a score crosses the **activation threshold** (60-80 depending on archetype), the city gains that identity. Cities can have 1-3 active archetypes simultaneously (e.g., "Tourist Paradise + Cultural Capital").

### 4.2 The 20 Archetypes

---

#### 1. TOURIST PARADISE
**Conditions:**
- Climate: Tropical, Subtropical, or Mediterranean
- Terrain: Coastal or Island
- Tourism buildings: 3+ hotels, 2+ attractions (beach, resort, theme park, etc.)
- Safety: Crime rate < 5 per 1,000
- Tourist satisfaction > 70
- Cultural DNA: Work Ethic < 55 (relaxed vibe)

**Bonuses:** Tourism revenue +40%, immigration from wealthy retirees, international visibility
**Challenges:** Seasonal economy (off-peak slump), housing prices spike (Airbnb effect), overtourism risk, service worker shortage (low wages vs. high cost of living)
**Visual Changes:** Beach umbrellas, cocktail bars, tourist buses, souvenir shops, palm-lined promenades, cruise ships in harbor
**UI Badge:** Palm tree icon, "Tourist Paradise" title
**Can evolve into:** Sin City (relax vice laws), Retirement Haven (attract retirees), Cultural Capital (invest in heritage)

## 5. CULTURAL DRIFT MECHANICS

Cultural DNA is not static -- it shifts over time based on what happens in the city.

### 5.1 Drift Sources

| Source | Speed | Direction | Example |
|--------|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Immigration** | Moderate | Toward immigrant culture | 10,000 immigrants from high-trust culture -> city trust rises |
| **Education level** | Slow | Progressivism +, Risk Tolerance + | University-educated population trends progressive |
| **Economic prosperity** | Moderate | Risk Tolerance +, Trust + | Boom times = more risk-taking, more trust |
| **Economic hardship** | Fast | Trust -, Risk Tolerance - | Recession = distrust, conservatism |
| **Generational change** | Very slow | Variable by era | Each generation trends slightly different from parents |
| **Media exposure** | Moderate | Toward media bias | TV/internet homogenizes some values, amplifies others |
| **Policy feedback** | Slow | Reinforcing | Authoritarian policies -> higher hierarchy acceptance |
| **Traumatic events** | Instant | Variable | Pandemic -> collectivism +, War -> cultural pride + |
| **Globalization (Modern+)** | Moderate | Cultural Pride -, Progressivism + | Exposure to global culture reduces insularity |
| **Religious institutions** | Slow | Tradition +, Hierarchy + | Strong religious presence reinforces traditional values |

### 5.2 Drift Formula

```
monthly_drift[dimension] = sum_of_all_sources(
  source_strength * source_direction * exposure_factor
) / population_inertia

population_inertia = population_size * (1 + average_age / 50)
// Larger and older populations resist change more
```

### 5.3 Sudden Shift Events

Some events cause immediate cultural shifts:

| Event | Dimension Affected | Shift |
|-------|-------------------|-------|
| Major terrorist attack | Social Trust | -15 instantly |
| Economic miracle | Risk Tolerance | +10 over 6 months |
| Pandemic | Collectivism | +12 instantly |
| War / military conflict | Cultural Pride | +15, Hierarchy +10 |
| Civil rights movement | Progressivism | +10 over 2 years |
| Environmental disaster | Environmental Values | +12 instantly |
| Corruption scandal | Social Trust | -8 instantly |
| Space program achievement | Risk Tolerance | +5, Cultural Pride +5 |
| Olympic hosting | Cultural Pride | +8 |
| Religious revival | Progressivism | -10, Cultural Pride +5 |

## 6. CROSS-SYSTEM INTEGRATION

### 6.1 Culture -> Politics
```
law_acceptance[law] = base_support
  + culture_alignment_bonus     // Does the law match cultural values?
  - culture_opposition_penalty  // Does it conflict with cultural values?
  + media_influence             // Is media framing it positively?
  + economic_impact_perception  // Do citizens think it helps economy?

// Example: Speed camera law in high-trust culture
law_acceptance["speed_cameras"] = 40 + (social_trust - 50) * 0.3 + (collectivism - 50) * 0.2
// High trust (75) + collectivist (70) = 40 + 7.5 + 4 = 51.5% support (passes)
// Low trust (30) + individualist (30) = 40 - 6 - 4 = 30% support (fails)
```

### 6.2 Culture -> Economy
```
// Industry viability affected by culture
industry_productivity[sector] = base_productivity
  * work_ethic_modifier         // High work ethic = more output
  * cultural_match_bonus        // Cultural pride city resists foreign chains

// Entrepreneurship rate
startup_rate = base_rate * (risk_tolerance / 50) * (1 + education_level * 0.3)
// High risk tolerance (80) = 1.6x startup rate
// Low risk tolerance (30) = 0.6x startup rate
```

### 6.3 Culture -> Transport
```
// Mode share influenced by culture
transit_acceptance = base * (collectivism / 50) * (1 - car_culture_weight)
car_preference = base * (individualism / 50) * (1 + highway_investment)
cycling_acceptance = base * (env_values / 50) * (progressivism / 50)

// Individualist culture: 70% car, 20% transit, 10% walk/bike (without intervention)
// Collectivist culture: 40% car, 40% transit, 20% walk/bike (without intervention)
```

### 6.4 Culture -> Demographics
```
// Birth rate cultural modifier
birth_rate_modifier = 1.0
  + (traditional - 50) * 0.005   // Traditional cultures have more children
  + (collectivism - 50) * 0.003  // Family-oriented cultures
  - (progressivism - 50) * 0.004 // Progressive cultures have fewer children

// Immigration acceptance
immigration_flow_modifier = 1.0
  - (cultural_pride - 50) * 0.01  // Proud cultures resist immigration
  + (progressivism - 50) * 0.008  // Progressive cultures welcome diversity
  + (risk_tolerance - 50) * 0.005 // Risk-tolerant cultures open to change
```

### 6.5 Culture -> Events
```
// Event probability modified by culture
event_probability["labor_strike"] *= (1 - hierarchy_acceptance / 100) * 2
// Low hierarchy = strong unions = more strikes

event_probability["environmental_protest"] *= (env_values / 50)
// High env values = more environmental protests

event_probability["religious_festival"] *= (cultural_pride / 50) * (tradition / 50)
// High pride + traditional = more religious events
```

## 7. PLAYER FEEDBACK SYSTEMS

### 7.1 City Identity Panel (UI)

Main screen shows:
- **Radar chart**: 8 cultural dimensions visualized as octagon
- **Active archetypes**: 1-3 badges with icons and names
- **Archetype trajectory**: Arrow showing which archetype you're trending toward
- **Drift indicators**: Small arrows on each dimension showing current trend direction
- **Global reputation**: How other cities/players perceive you (affects trade, immigration, tourism)

### 7.2 Cultural Heatmap Overlay
- Per-neighborhood cultural values visualized as heatmap
- Shows clustering: "This neighborhood is 80% collectivist" vs "This one is 60% individualist"
- Shows ethnic/cultural diversity index per block
- Shows gentrification pressure (where values are shifting fast)

### 7.3 Advisor Cultural Commentary
Advisors reference culture in their advice:
- Transport advisor: "Citizens here prefer personal vehicles. Transit investment will need heavy marketing."
- Economy advisor: "Our risk-tolerant culture is attracting startups. We should capitalize with a tech zone."
- Social advisor: "Cultural pride is very high. Foreign chain stores will face resistance."
- Environment advisor: "Environmental values are rising with the younger generation. Green policies will find support."

### 7.4 Visual Cultural Cues
- **Architecture style**: Changes based on culture + era (log cabins for frontier, minarets for Middle Eastern, pagodas for East Asian, brownstones for European)
- **Citizen clothing**: Pixel art clothing reflects culture (suits in financial center, casual in artist colony, traditional in holy city)
- **Street life**: Animation patterns reflect culture (crowded market haggling, quiet orderly queues, bicycle commuters, food truck gatherings)
- **Signage language**: Street signs and shop names reflect dominant culture/language
- **Public behavior**: Citizen animations (bowing in hierarchical cultures, handshakes in egalitarian, street vendors in collectivist)

### 7.5 City Reputation Score
```
reputation = weighted_average(
  archetype_prestige:      30%  // Tech Hub/Financial Center = high prestige
  safety:                  20%  // Low crime = good reputation
  environment:             15%  // Clean air/water = positive image
  cultural_output:         15%  // Museums, festivals, heritage
  economic_strength:       10%  // GDP per capita
  international_events:    10%  // Olympics, World Cup, expos
)
```

Reputation affects:
- Immigration quality (higher reputation attracts wealthier, more educated immigrants)
- Tourism volume
- Trade deal terms (better reputation = better prices)
- Foreign investment attraction
- Multiplayer diplomatic influence

## 8. FEEDBACK LOOPS

### 8.1 Positive Feedback Loops (Virtuous Cycles)

**The Innovation Attractor:**
Tech companies -> talent attraction -> talent attracts more companies -> university graduates stay -> VC investment -> successful exits -> angel investors fund next wave -> reputation attracts international talent
*Break condition: housing crisis, quality of life collapse, competing city*

**The Green Virtuous Cycle:**
Green policies pass -> air quality improves -> health improves -> happiness rises -> eco-conscious immigrants arrive -> more green policies supported -> green business attraction -> eco-tourism revenue
*Break condition: economic crisis forces cost-cutting*

**The Trust Builder:**
Good services -> citizen satisfaction -> tax compliance -> more revenue -> better services -> trust increases -> more cooperation -> less crime -> even better quality of life
*Break condition: corruption scandal, service cut*

### 8.2 Negative Feedback Loops (Death Spirals)

**The Brain Drain Spiral:**
Unemployment -> educated workers leave -> education level drops -> innovation decreases -> fewer startups -> more unemployment -> social trust drops -> crime rises -> more emigration
*Break condition: massive external investment, university construction*

**The Trust Collapse:**
Corruption revealed -> trust drops -> tax evasion increases -> revenue drops -> services deteriorate -> more dissatisfaction -> crime increases -> more distrust -> political extremism
*Break condition: anti-corruption crackdown, charismatic leader event*

**The Gentrification Engine:**
High-income workers arrive -> property values rise -> low-income residents displaced -> neighborhood culture shifts -> new businesses cater to wealthy -> more price increase -> artists/creatives leave -> area loses character -> next cheap area gets targeted
*Break condition: rent control, affordable housing mandate, community land trust*

**The Aging Trap:**
Seniors attract healthcare investment -> more seniors move in -> young people find city boring -> leave -> average age increases -> conservative cultural drift -> less appeal to young immigrants -> workforce shrinks -> service worker shortage -> economic stagnation
*Break condition: university investment, tech-for-seniors industry*

## 10. MULTIPLAYER CULTURAL INTERACTIONS

### 10.1 Cultural Trade
- Cities trade not just goods but **cultural influence**
- A Tech Hub trading with an Agricultural Heartland slowly shifts the heartland's risk tolerance upward
- Cultural exports (music, film, fashion) from a Cultural Capital influence connected cities

### 10.2 Immigration Flows
- Citizens migrate between player cities based on job opportunities, quality of life, and cultural compatibility
- A liberal progressive citizen is more likely to migrate to another progressive city than a traditional one
- Mass migration events can dramatically shift a receiving city's cultural DNA

### 10.3 Diplomatic Reputation
- Your city's archetype affects how other player cities perceive you
- Tech Hub + Financial Center = "power ally" (everyone wants trade deals)
- Sin City + Panopticon = "pariah" (diplomatic penalties)
- Green Utopia + Cultural Capital = "prestige" (cultural influence bonus)

### 10.4 Cultural Clashes
- When cities with very different cultural DNA have high immigration between them, integration challenges emerge
- Player can set immigration policy: open borders, selective, points-based, cultural requirements
- Diaspora communities form in receiving cities, creating cultural diversity (tourism boost) but also integration costs
