# ADR: Meshy.ai Asset Pipeline for CityMajor

**Status:** Proposed  
**Linear:** SB-3678  
**Date:** 2026-07-04  
**Context:** Integration of Meshy.ai Text-to-3D API for generating the ~500 mid-fidelity building meshes required by the R3F web client, complying with `BUILDING_ARCHETYPE_3D.md`.

---

## 1. Modular Building Workflow & R3F Integration

CityMajor uses a dynamic story count system (`ComputeStories`). To avoid window/texture stretching when scaling vertically:
- **Unit Height:** Meshy will be prompted to generate **single-story, block-shaped modules** or low-height buildings that fit cleanly into a 1x1x1 bounding box.
- **Modularity:** For mid-fidelity city builders, the best practice is to generate a ground floor, a repeatable middle floor, and a roof cap. However, given the API's single-prompt nature, we will generate the **whole building at 1-story** and handle roof archetype swapping or Y-scaling procedurally in R3F, OR generate "base/mid/roof" sub-components.
- **Current Approach:** We will generate unit-story building blocks per the `BUILDING_ARCHETYPE_3D.md` taxonomy. 
- **Pivot Point:** The GLTF meshes generated by Meshy must have their pivot point adjusted to the **bottom-center** (ground center). Since Meshy's native export centers the bounding box, a post-processing script (e.g., using `gltf-transform` or Three.js in-engine) will shift the geometry up by `BoundingBox.Y / 2`.

## 2. Naming Convention

All generated assets strictly follow the `BUILDING_ARCHETYPE_3D.md` format:
`{categoryPrefix}_{eraSlug}_{variant}.glb`

Examples:
- `res_low_frontier_00.glb`
- `com_industrial_15.glb`
- `ind_future_07.glb`

## 3. Meshy.ai API & Pricing (2026)

Meshy.ai uses a two-step REST API:
1. **Preview (`mode: "preview"`):** Generates untextured geometry.
   - Cost: **20 credits** (Meshy-6 model).
2. **Refine (`mode: "refine"`):** Applies textures to the preview ID.
   - Cost: **10 credits**.

**Total Cost per Asset:** 30 credits.
For the full taxonomy of 500 zone meshes + service variants, the batch will require ~15,000+ credits.

## 4. Best Practices for Mid-Fidelity City Builder Assets

- **Art Style:** "Isometric city builder, mid-fidelity, low poly, stylized, clean textures, matte colors, ambient occlusion baked."
- **Camera Angle:** The models will be viewed top-down isometric. Prompts should specify "viewed from above, emphasized roof details."
- **Topology:** Ask for "low poly optimization, blocky structure" to ensure rendering performance when instancing thousands of buildings in R3F.

## 5. Prompts per Archetype

Below are the base prompt templates for the archetype combinations:

### Residential Low (`res_low`)
- **Frontier:** "A single-story frontier log cabin, colonial era house, wooden planks, peaked roof, low poly, stylized game asset, isometric view."
- **Industrial:** "A single-story 1800s brick rowhouse, industrial era, pitched roof, low poly, stylized game asset, matte colors."
- **Postwar:** "A single-story 1950s suburban house, postwar architecture, simple peaked roof, clean stylized, low poly."
- **Modern:** "A single-story contemporary house, modern architecture, peaked roof, wood and stucco, stylized game asset, low poly."
- **Future:** "A single-story sci-fi eco house, futuristic architecture, solar panels, sleek design, peaked roof, low poly, stylized."

### Residential High (`res_high`)
*(Prompted as dense, repeating structures)*
- **Industrial:** "A dense 19th-century tenement building block, brick texture, flat roof with lip, low poly, stylized game asset."
- **Modern:** "A modern mid-rise apartment block, balconies, flat roof with lip, concrete and glass, low poly, stylized game asset."

### Commercial (`com`)
- **Frontier:** "A frontier general store, wooden facade, flat roof with lip, wild west style, low poly, stylized game asset."
- **Modern:** "A modern glass storefront retail building, flat roof with lip and HVAC units, low poly, stylized game asset."

### Industrial (`ind`)
- **Postwar:** "A postwar factory building, sawtooth roof, brick and metal, loading docks, low poly, stylized game asset."
- **Future:** "A futuristic automated manufacturing plant, neon accents, sawtooth roof, metal panels, low poly, stylized."

## 6. Export Settings

When hitting the Meshy API, use the following payload constraints:
- `target_formats: ["glb"]`
- `art_style: "realistic"` (or stylized depending on Meshy version options, but prompting heavily drives the output).
- `topology: "quad"` (if available, for easier R3F subdivision/scaling, otherwise stick to default triangle decimation).

A post-processing step will download the `.glb` files into `web/public/assets/gltf/`.
