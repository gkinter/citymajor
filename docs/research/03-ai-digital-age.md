### What the research covers:

**1. Internet Age (1990-2010)** -- E-commerce grew from 3.86% of retail (2000) to 18.4% (2024), killing ~1,800 malls. 64 million Americans now do gig work. Data centers consume 25% of Virginia's electricity. Dead malls became mixed-use (46%), warehouses (9%), housing (7%), or medical facilities (4%).

**2. AI Age (2020+)** -- McKinsey projects 30% of US work hours automatable by 2030. Office support, customer service, and clerical jobs go first; trades and healthcare are most resilient. Three major UBI experiments (Finland, Stockton, Kenya) all showed employment *increased* (not decreased), mental health improved dramatically, and 99%+ of money went to essentials. AI data centers will consume 945 TWh by 2030 (equivalent to Japan). Waymo does 150,000 driverless trips/week. Predictive policing was abandoned in LA and Chicago after reinforcing racial bias.

**3. Remote Work** -- US office vacancy hit 20.9% (nearly doubled from pre-COVID). BART ridership is only 40% of pre-pandemic levels. Boise grew 3.7%/year as a "Zoom town." Co-working spaces grew to 7,814 US locations ($41.4B global market).

**4. Climate Tech** -- Norway hit 93% EV market share; US stalled at ~10%. Copenhagen failed its 2025 carbon-neutral goal and shifted to 2035. Vertical farming produces 10-100x yields per m^2 but costs 5-10x more and uses 30-60x more energy. Amsterdam achieved 95% construction waste recycling in pilot zones and cut construction CO2 by 32%.

**5. Smart City** -- Pittsburgh's AI traffic lights delivered a real 25% travel-time reduction. Predictive maintenance yields 18-25% cost reduction. Sidewalk Labs Toronto failed due to privacy/trust concerns. Helsinki's participatory budgeting had 47,064 voters allocating EUR8.8M.

**6. Crypto/Fintech** -- Bitcoin consumes 137-183 TWh/year. Texas crypto miners can draw 2,600 MW (= Austin's entire power use). Nigeria's eNaira has 28.5M wallets but only 197K active. China's digital yuan processed 14.2 trillion yuan (3.32B transactions, 225M wallets). Blockchain land registries: Georgia succeeded, Sweden piloted, Honduras failed.
